## Review criteria

1. The appearance must carry the primary reference's continuous sidebar, neutral layers, system type, quiet selection, inset rows, and compact familiar controls.
2. No industrial bevels, ambient glow, rainbow composer border, decorative gradients, or nested decorative card stacks may dominate the affected surfaces.
3. Labels, values, separators and control alignment must stay clear at the supported default and minimum native widths. Long descriptions wrap; selections remain readable.
4. Navigation stays visible while content scrolls. Chat remains usable with both resizable side panels present, and execution state is visible without decorative motion.
5. Existing data, callbacks, keyboard navigation, lazy loading, provider forms and confirmation flows remain intact. Structural loading states and accessible status names remain.
6. The user subsequently requires white navigation glyphs throughout Settings, the content scrollbar at the window edge, and two or three columns for independent tool/catalog panels when the actual content width permits. This feedback supersedes the earlier full-width treatment of those independent cards.
7. A cold launch must open the main window maximized within the monitor's usable desktop area, without entering fullscreen or leaving the reported gap above the window. Manual restoration must retain the existing normal bounds.
